Built for the field

Every feature designed around one constraint: zero connectivity. Your data never leaves the device unless you choose to export it.

GPS-Tagged Issue Tracking

Project-based organization with precise GPS coordinates for every issue. Classify severity from cosmetic to critical with custom workflows.

Native Photo Annotation

Capture with the native camera and annotate with 5 precision tools: arrow, circle, rectangle, freehand pen, and text labels.

Pre-Loaded Inspection Templates

4 encrypted inspection templates ready out of the box. Structural, electrical, safety, and environmental checklists with zero setup.

Branded PDF Reports

Generate professional reports with 8 branded templates. Password-encrypted PDF output that clients and regulators trust.

AES-256-GCM Encryption

Field data encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. Unlock with biometric authentication — Face ID, fingerprint, or device PIN.

Device Migration Wizard

Encrypted backup and restore with a step-by-step migration wizard. Move your entire project history to a new device in minutes.

See it in action

Clean, focused interfaces designed for gloves, bright sunlight, and one-handed operation on site.
