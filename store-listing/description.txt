Short description:
EverSiteAudit — Streamlined site inspections, issue tracking, and professional reporting on the go.

Full description:
EverSiteAudit is the all-in-one mobile companion for construction, facilities, and site audit professionals. Capture issues, annotate photos, manage projects, and generate polished PDF reports — all from your phone or tablet.

Key features:
• Project Management — Create and organize site audit projects in one place.
• Issue Tracking — Log issues with severity, status, and detailed descriptions.
• Photo Annotation — Capture and mark up photos directly in the app.
• Gallery & PDF Preview — Review media and export-ready reports before sharing.
• Professional Export — Generate and share PDF reports with stakeholders instantly.
• Audit Templates — Speed up recurring inspections with reusable templates.
• Secure & Offline-Ready — Your data stays protected and accessible, even on remote sites.

Whether you're conducting building inspections, safety audits, or quality assurance reviews, EverSiteAudit keeps your workflow efficient and your documentation impeccable.
