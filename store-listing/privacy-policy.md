# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 14, 2026

## Introduction

EverSiteAudit ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the EverSiteAudit mobile application.

## Our Core Privacy Promise

**EverSiteAudit is designed to be completely offline and privacy-first:**

- **All data stays on your device.** Your projects, issues, photos, and annotations are stored locally in an encrypted SQLite database on your phone or tablet.
- **No analytics or telemetry.** We do not track your usage, collect crash logs, or monitor your behavior in any way.
- **No cloud sync or third-party services.** We do not transmit your data to any server, cloud infrastructure, or third party. The app functions entirely without network connectivity.
- **No account required.** You do not need to create an account, provide an email address, or share any personal information to use the app.
- **You control your data.** All exports, backups, and sharing are initiated by you and only you.

## Information Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored **locally and only locally** on your device:

- **Project and audit data** — project names, locations, client information, issue descriptions, status, severity, and annotations
- **Photos and media** — images captured or imported for your inspections
- **Application preferences** — theme settings, biometric lock preferences, and inspector profile
- **Exported reports** — PDF, ZIP, JSON, and CSV files you explicitly generate and choose to share

## Data Security

- Your sensitive data is protected with **AES-256-GCM encryption** at rest.
- Encryption keys are stored in your device's secure hardware (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).
- Optional **biometric authentication** (Face ID, Touch ID, or Fingerprint) adds an additional layer of protection.
- Backups you create are encrypted with a passphrase of your choosing.

## Data Sharing

**We do not and cannot access your data.** Because all data remains on your device:

- We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone.
- We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
- We do not process your data on our servers — we have no servers.

## Your Choices

- You can delete your projects, issues, and photos at any time within the app.
- You can create encrypted backups to migrate your data to a new device.
- You can enable or disable biometric lock and field encryption in Settings.
- You can export your data in multiple formats (PDF, ZIP, JSON, CSV) for your own records or to share with stakeholders.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app and the effective date will be updated. Because the app does not collect data, changes to this policy are primarily for clarity and transparency.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the app's privacy practices, please contact us through the support channels listed in the app store listing.
